Prof. [Tong Zhang](https://arte-lab.github.io/members/Tong-Zhang.html) and [Zhaozhi Wang](https://arte-lab.github.io/members/Zhaozhi-Wang.html) were invited to participate in CICV 2026, the 13th Congress of Intelligent and Connected Vehicles Technology, held in Shanghai from May 21 to May 22, 2026.

During the A2 workshop, *World Model: A New Paradigm for Autonomous Driving Research from High-Quality Data Generation to Reinforcement Learning Training*, Zhaozhi delivered a talk on behalf of Prof. Zhang's team. The presentation, titled *Exploring Safety for Autonomous Driving: From Scene Generation to Planning*, discussed how scenario generation and planning methods can support safer autonomous-driving systems. The A2 program lists Prof. Zhang's invited talk in the workshop schedule, and the session materials are available in the [CICV 2026 A2 program](/news-imgs/cicv/CICV2026程序册-A2世界模型部分.pdf).

Congratulations to Prof. Zhang, Zhaozhi, and the team on sharing our autonomous-driving research with the CICV community.
